# 🏚️ BREAK IN: THORNTON (104th Ave)

**"A quiet night in Colorado turns into a fight for survival."**

Inspired by the legendary gameplay style of **Cracky4**, *Break In: Thornton* is a 3D survival horror experience built for the web. You and your group have taken shelter in a house on 104th Avenue, but you aren't alone.

---

## 📖 The Story
The news reports were right. A strange surge of "Break Ins" has been sweeping through Thornton, Colorado. You've managed to find a safe house, but the sun is setting fast. You have 60 seconds of daylight left to explore the house and find a place to hide. 

When the timer hits zero... **The Thornton Shadows** come out to play. Will you survive the night?

---

## 🎮 Features
- **3D Movement:** Explore the house with classic WASD controls.
- **Real-time Day/Night Cycle:** Watch the sky turn black as the threat approaches.
- **Thornton Landmarks:** Built with the vibe of 104th Ave in mind.
- **Survival Mechanics:** Manage your HP and keep an eye on the timer.
